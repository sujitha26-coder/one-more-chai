Deliverables 8 & 9 • Compliance & Project Conclusion

Verification Report, Tool Licensing & Project Conclusion

Directly addressing the central Capstone 53 research question with empirical findings, free-tier tool licensing terms, and responsible AI governance.

Capstone Core Question Answered

"Can AI tools help create distinctive logo concepts and a style guide?"

Conclusion: YES, with Human-in-the-Loop Vector Refinement. While raw generative AI tools (Leonardo, Bing, ChatGPT) excel at conceptual synthesis, rapid divergent ideation (generating 50+ aesthetic directions in under 15 minutes), and semantic style guide drafting, they cannot autonomously deliver production-grade final logos due to raster pixelation, asymmetry hallucinations, and lack of deterministic vector grids. The optimal methodology is a hybrid workflow: AI ideation → human vector precision in Canva → automated style guide documentation.
